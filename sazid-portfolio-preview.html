Short version

Who I am, without the padding

I started building companies before finishing school, and I kept both going at once. SAZ Corporation is the venture I founded and lead. Armani is the one I co-founded with a partner. Between them I handle the parts most people avoid — pricing, suppliers, cash flow, and the awkward conversations that decide whether a young business survives its first year.

Chattogram is a port city, so trade is in the water here. That shapes how I work: margins over noise, relationships over pitches, and something real shipped before anything is announced.

How I work

Four rules I actually follow

Every venture gets tested against these before it gets my time.

Sell it before you build it

If nobody will pay for the idea in conversation, they will not pay for it in a shop.

Own the supply chain

Margins die in the middle. I keep sourcing close and relationships direct.

Small team, clear roles

A few people who know exactly what they own beats a crowd waiting on instructions.

Study, then ship

College is not on pause. Discipline moves between the two in both directions.
